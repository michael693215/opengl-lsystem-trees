All textures come from cc0textures.com with the following changes

- *-color.ppm: Converted to ppm and resized to 512x512
  - Used ImageMagick: "magick color.png -resize 512x512 color.ppm"
- *-normal.ppm: For textures with only DX map, Green channel is inverted per OpenGL convention
  - Used ImageMagick: "magick normal.png -channel G -negate -resize 512x512 normal.ppm"
- *-props.ppm: Roughness, Displacement, and AmbientOcclusion maps packed into *-props.ppm
  - Used ImageMagick: "magick rough.png disp.png ao.png -combine -resize 512x512 props.ppm"
